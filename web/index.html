Quazaar Synker

The ultimate bridge between your Android device, Apple Music, and your Linux desktop environment.

View on GitHub
📱

Android Client App

A beautifully designed background service that hooks into your Android notification listener to securely extract live playback data from Apple Music, Spotify, and more.

⚡

Go Daemon

A lightning-fast Linux daemon that receives encrypted websocket payloads from your phone, stores the playback history in a local SQLite database, and exposes REST APIs.

📊

Live Command Center

An embedded web UI showcasing your real-time listening activity, complete with album artwork extraction, day-by-day play history, and Apple Music Replay analytics.

🔌

MPRIS Integration

Acts as a virtual media player on your Linux desktop. Pause, skip, or view what's playing on your phone directly from your desktop panel via D-Bus MPRIS specifications.

How It Works

Your Android device runs the Synker Client, intercepting media notifications and securely sending P2P UDP broadcasts and WebSocket events to the Synker Daemon running on your Linux host. The Daemon logs the data to SQLite, exposes a WebUI, and proxies the controls via MPRIS.
